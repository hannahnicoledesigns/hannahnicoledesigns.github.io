About

Hi, I'm Hannah <3

As a creative at heart, I started crocheting as a mindful alternative to scrolling and as a productive way of keeping my hands busy, and I quickly found it to be the perfect creative outlet alongside my academic studies at uni.

I love how each piece is built from hundreds or thousands of deliberate little stitches, and how every new design is a chance to deepen my understanding of the craft and what I can create.

That love for the process is what led me here: designing my own crochet patterns. The encouragement I received from sharing my crochet online gave me the confidence to start turning my ideas into patterns, and I'm so excited to share the pieces I've been dreaming up with you! Thank you for being here <3

FAQ

What skill level are your patterns?

Most patterns will be written for ambitious beginners/intermediate crocheters. Skill level will be noted on every pattern page.

How do I receive my pattern?

Patterns are sold as instant PDF digital downloads through Etsy and Ko-fi.

Can I sell items made from your patterns?

Feel free to sell items made from my patterns, all I ask is that you make sure to give credit and directions to my page/social media. The patterns themselves can not be resold or reproduced in part or in whole.

Intermediate / ambitious beginner

Inigo Flowers Lampshade

A filet-crochet lampshade cover in a delicate floral motif, worked in 4-ply yarn. Designed for a close friend of mine, Inigo, hence the name, and now released out into the world. The open and filled squares catch the light beautifully around the flowers.

Well suited to intermediate crocheters, and a perfect project for your first time trying filet crochet thanks to the full guide included.

Full materials list, yardage, hook size and lampshade sizing are included in the pattern description on Etsy and Ko-fi.
